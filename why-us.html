How we work

A disciplined process, from brief to offer.

Each search is built around your requirements, with a recruiter accountable for progress and communication at every stage.

01

Define the brief

We align on responsibilities, must-have skills, experience, location, budget, and target timeline.

02

Confirm the plan

Before work begins, we agree the search approach, applicable fees, commitment amount, and relevant terms.

03

Search with purpose

Your recruiter uses relevant channels and networks to reach talent beyond incoming applications.

04

Screen against criteria

We review candidates’ experience and skills against the requirements agreed with your team.

05

Share a focused shortlist

Review relevant profiles with useful context, then coordinate interviews, feedback, and follow-ups.

06

Support the close

We help coordinate candidate communication, offer discussions, joining steps, and any agreed replacement support.

A transparent partnership

Know the terms before the search begins.

We discuss the commercial details upfront and confirm them in writing, so responsibilities and expectations are clear from day one.

Clear fees

Applicable recruitment fees and the commitment amount are agreed before the search begins. The commitment amount is adjusted against the final invoice as agreed.

No hire, no success fee

A success fee applies when you hire a candidate introduced through the search. If you do not hire, no success fee is due; any commitment fee treatment is confirmed in writing.

Support after placement

Any replacement support, eligibility conditions, and timelines are set out in the agreed terms before the search begins.
