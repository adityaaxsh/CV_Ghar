About CV Ghar

Recruitment for teams ready to grow.

CV Ghar partners with startups and growing businesses to source and assess talent across technology, sales, marketing, operations, finance, and more—with a clear recruiter-led process from brief to offer.

We take time to understand each role, then bring together targeted sourcing, recruiter-led screening, and practical interview coordination. Your team gets a clear point of contact and regular updates throughout the search.

Our Vision

Help growing businesses make thoughtful hires through structured searches, relevant shortlists, and clear communication.

Our Mission

Make recruitment easier to navigate for employers and candidates, while helping teams find talent that fits the role.
